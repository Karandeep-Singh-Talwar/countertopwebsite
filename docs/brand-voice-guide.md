# Verona Surfaces · Brand Voice & Message Guide

## Brand Tagline
> **Continuous stone. Made for living.**

---

## Brand Positioning Story
Verona surfaces bring the quiet weight of quarried stone into modern homes without the fragile upkeep. One continuous mineral pattern flows from your kitchen island, up the backsplash, and across the floor. Cut and set by your local contractor with standard tools, delivered directly from our kilns to your door.

---

## Five Voice Principles

### Principle 1: Paint the picture, skip the praise
Concrete visual details help homeowners see their room. Abstract luxury adjectives sound like sales copy.
- **We say:** "Soft charcoal river veins across clean milk-white."
- **We don’t say:** "Stunning Italian marble elegance of breathtaking beauty."

### Principle 2: Speak in household truth, not lab metrics
Homeowners care about Friday night wine spills and hot sheet pans, not test standards.
- **We say:** "Red wine, citrus, and cooking oil wipe clean with water."
- **We don’t say:** "Certified under 0.04% vitrification for stain-free performance."

### Principle 3: Frame direct pricing with calm clarity, never discount language
Our advantage is direct access, honest numbers, and no showroom markup. We never sound cheap.
- **We say:** "Direct from the source. Open itemized quotes dispatched in minutes."
- **We don’t say:** "Cut out the middleman and save 60% with affordable budget slabs!"

### Principle 4: Make installation sound approachable, not intimidating
Homeowners fear endless fabricator delays and specialist fees. Show them their existing crew can handle it.
- **We say:** "Installed by your tile contractor or general contractor with standard tools."
- **We don’t say:** "Requires specialized bridge saw CNC diamond cutting and heavy handling."

### Principle 5: State facts with quiet confidence
A brand that knows the strength of its material never needs exclamation points or hype.
- **We say:** "Set boiling cookware straight off the stove. You will not scorch it."
- **We don’t say:** "Experience our game-changing, revolutionary heat protection!"
